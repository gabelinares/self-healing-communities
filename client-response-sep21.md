# Draft response to Laura — Sept 21 (Our Approach page rebuilt)

Hi Laura,

The Our Approach page is rebuilt, live on both versions.

**The spiral is the page now.** It used to sit below the headline, with the explanation below that, so you could never see the diagram and the words about it at the same time. Everything is on one screen now — the spiral on the right, and whatever step you're on written out on the left. Nothing about the cycle needs a scroll.

**The step names read straight.** They used to tilt to follow the curve, which looked handsome and was genuinely hard to read at the bottom of the circle. They're horizontal now, wherever they land.

**It looks like something you operate.** The steps are buttons — they lift when you point at one, the chosen one goes dark green, and the path lights from the centre out to it. Six markers under the text show where you are in the six, and work as a second way in if you don't think to point at the diagram.

**And every few seconds a pulse of light runs the spiral.** It comes out of the centre and fades as it travels out. It's there so the page reads as alive before anyone touches it, and it steps aside the moment you open a step, so it never competes with the path you're looking at.

Both versions have this, so you're still comparing the same page — only the type differs. And the A/B switch is now on this page too, not just the homepage: last time, following "See our approach" from version B left you stranded with no way back.
