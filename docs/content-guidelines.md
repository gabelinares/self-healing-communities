# Content Guidelines

**All SHCF copy must follow Laura's definition below.** She wrote it in June 2026, after rejecting
an earlier hero line ("communities that heal themselves") as the *opposite* of what she means.

## Laura's definition of "Self-Healing Communities"

> The name Self-Healing Communities comes from the observation that healthy ecosystems,
> organizations, families, and communities all possess natural capacities for adaptation and renewal.
>
> A forest heals after a fire. A body heals after an injury. A community can recover from adversity.
> None of these happen "by themselves." They happen because living systems contain relationships,
> resources, feedback loops, and regenerative processes that support recovery and growth.
>
> Self-healing is about families, communities, and funders **participating more effectively in a
> living system**. Investments are aligned to equip people and communities to grow stronger together
> and sustain wellbeing over time. Effectiveness improves when we "see" differently – when we
> understand causal factors that are interrupting the self-healing processes in a community. It is
> about taking informed and highly strategic action to promote experiences that generate cascades of
> positive effects over the life course while compassionately reducing developmental adversity from
> one generation to the next.
>
> As communities become more connected and effective, they create positive cycles of health, safety,
> and wellbeing. Children experience fewer Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs), which can reduce
> later mental health challenges, violence, addiction, and other costly problems. In this way, each
> generation can leave the next generation stronger, healthier, and better prepared to flourish.
>
> We cannot force a river to flow. But we can remove barriers, strengthen riverbanks, reconnect
> channels, and restore the conditions that allow water to move where it naturally wants to go.
>
> In the same way, we do not create wellbeing by fixing one problem at a time. The Self-Healing
> Communities Fund helps communities, in partnership with government and philanthropic funders,
> create conditions that allow health, resilience, connection, and opportunity to flow through
> families, neighborhoods, organizations, and generations.
>
> Healing emerges from the system as a whole; it is not the work of any single program, profession,
> or agency; it is the work of the living system we call "community".

## Do / don't

**Never write:** "heal themselves", "by themselves", "designed to heal". These read as automatic or
engineered, which is wrong.

**Do use:** living system · participate more effectively · remove barriers / restore conditions ·
let health, resilience, connection and opportunity flow · see differently · cascades over the life
course · reduce ACEs and developmental adversity across generations · each generation leaves the
next stronger · in partnership with government and philanthropic funders · healing emerges from the
whole system, not one program.

## Tone

Plain language, warm, concrete. The audience is around 60 and reading carefully: short sentences,
no jargon without explanation, details one click away on a separate page rather than crammed onto
the homepage.
