### Oral tradition / elder storytelling concept (Krista)
Krista raised expanding beyond the book metaphor to include **elder storytelling, oral history, circle-gathering imagery** — communities where knowledge is passed in circles, not linearly. References:
- Elder in a town square with children gathered around
- Circle-oriented, organic, passing-on energy
- Diversity: many U.S. communities are elder-driven, story-driven cultures

Gabe's response: go **abstract** — use circular patterns, icons, and copy to evoke oral tradition alongside the book. The two can coexist.

Tagline idea surfaced by Krista: **"Gather around."**

### Hard concepts to convey (Laura)
Two concepts the team struggles to communicate and that the design should help address:

1. **Risk reduction before crisis** — the project works on invisible/preventive risk, not visible crisis response. Very hard for audiences to grasp.
2. **Framework-building, not direct service** — ACE's job is to create the conditions for communities to co-create their own systems. Not "we help children" but "we build the framework so communities can help children." Even core team members struggle with this.

Visual idea surfaced: **whiteboard/explainer animation** — a video where someone draws a concept and the illustration progresses in real time, optionally with voiceover. Could work well for explaining risk as it escalates into crisis.

### Content / copy
Laura acknowledged the team hasn't yet produced short, punchy headings/taglines for the site. They have a distributed team and won't meet in person until end of June. She will spend some time drafting these and sending them to Gabe.
