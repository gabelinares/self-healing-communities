# Client Feedback — Round 2 (post full-homepage mock, ~June 4)

Source: Laura forwarded async feedback from several team members after sharing the deployed homepage concept. Overall positive, but it surfaces a strategic pivot: the team is now describing a **full website**, not just a landing page.

## Positive reactions
- **Color scheme** — professional, calm, cohesive; stronger than the PPT slides.
- **Fonts** — clean and accessible.
- **Creativity** — movement and interactive elements make it engaging.
- Overall: visually engaging and creative.

## Specific issues to fix
1. **"An Ancient Story" (first section)** — only section that didn't render properly; book pages appear hidden behind the title. (May just be concept-vs-final.)
2. **Fonts behave oddly in some titles** — needs a pass.
3. **Content/visual mismatch** — e.g. "Each Stage Carries the One Before It" — the visual doesn't fully align with the content.

## The big strategic question (raised by multiple people)
**Will visitors understand what SHCF is early enough?** Risk of getting lost in content density or bailing halfway. The final slide is the clearest explanation of the fund — a similar orientation should appear near the **beginning** to ground people before the journey.

- "Too busy and complex." Everything is laid out in one place → overwhelming/messy.
- Wants: present **big ideas first**, then let people **click through to separate pages** for detail.
- Reference cited: **investinginflourishing.org** — tells you on the homepage what the effort is about, then details live on separate pages. (Team thinks our design is better but our organization/level-of-detail needs rethinking.)
- Likes the **hover-preview** feature on the Broad Institute site (already noted in round 1).

## Information architecture ideas from the team
- **Community stories as a separate tab** in main nav — could grow into its own destination.
- **Book-like format** for the "story call" (request for communities to submit stories).
- **Pop-ups / expandable definitions** for key concepts to orient visitors.
- Option to **attach a PDF** for more detailed info.

SHCF will need strong **data storytellers**. Data storytelling may be embedded in the site or housed elsewhere and linked. Examples shared (grade unknown):

Gabe's read: these are all about telling **one specific story/case well**. The open question — *which* story? One or many? Is the landing page a **hub for stories**? The team needs to supply content; the designer can't author the narrative alone.

## Gabe's strategic read (the pivot)
This feedback moves the project from **landing page → full website**. That changes everything downstream:
- New **information architecture / sitemap** is needed.
- Different **expected user behavior**: 30-second scan vs. minutes of reading. Need to define **user intent and audience**.
- The **vision of the website is not yet well-defined** — needs to be pinned down before more building.
- Relationship to the **existing aceinterface.com** site is unclear — landing page vs. full site have different goals.
- Need a defined **goal / call to action**: newsletter subscribe? institutional/branding only? what does the visitor do after reading?

Constraint: client team is ~60yo, not fast-paced tech adopters — needs gentle guidance, not jargon. Gabe has a **2h/day iteration window (~30 min of actual work)**, so guidance must be high-leverage and low-effort.
