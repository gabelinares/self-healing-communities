# Draft response to Laura — Sept 29 (the phone pass)

Hi Laura,

The site has had a full pass on phones — live at **hero-versions.vercel.app**.

Worth doing now because the link is being passed around, and a good share of the people you
send it to will open it on a handset. Several things were genuinely broken there and fine on a
laptop, so they hadn't shown up.

**The opening.** The intro paragraph was printing on top of the photograph and the headline ran
off both edges. Both fixed, and the picture has more room.

**The book.** Pages were flickering as they turned and sometimes showing the wrong picture —
page one showed the photographs instead of its quote. That's clean now.

**The life-course photograph.** It grows as the life unfolds; on a phone it now grows only
across, because growing taller pushed it up under the menu bar and squeezed the closing quote
off the bottom. The caption no longer cuts off on the longer stage names.

**The closing section.** The drifting dots were sitting on top of the words. They part around
the text now.

**The cycle diagram** is the one worth a look. It was the weakest thing on a phone — too small
to read, with the step names stripped out. It now stays on screen while the five steps scroll
underneath it, and each step lights its own point on the spiral as you reach it, with the path
drawing out from the centre. The steps are numbered on the diagram itself, so the order reads
standing still. It does on a phone what hovering does on a laptop.

Nothing changed on desktop.

One ask: open it on your own phone when you have a minute. Some of what I fixed is specific to
Safari on iPhone, and I can only test so far from here.

Gabe
