## Strategic Direction

We are working with **BIG change — bold ideas — and an unusual approach** to a problem that most people know about but don't know how to address.

### Content scope
Internal team had different opinions:
- Most prominent team member wants the site to focus on the **"what" and "how"** of the approach.
- Others wanted lots of resources posted for visitors — but the team is not ready to produce that content, and it may not be their job.
- Possible compromise: a section that **brokers resources offered by others** — but that content doesn't exist yet either.

### Tone & genre
Most of the reference sites are **not** social service or mental health sites. They are either:
- A balance of **art and science**, or
- Presenting **important ideas simply but boldly**.

### Narrative concept — "ancient story told in a new way"
The Fund is telling the **current life course and intergenerational story** — how people's lives unfold, and how we might strategically support that flow going in a healing direction.

Because it is a story, the client has been thinking about a **book metaphor**:
- **Pages** = life unfolding
- **Chapters** = developmental stages
- Possibly **multiple "books" within the book** = generations

### Visual anchor — accordion book installation
Client shared a photo of an art installation: an accordion/leporello book unfolding across a long table in a gallery, with visitors leaning in to read. Pages in warm tones (cream, orange, red) cascade in a continuous fold.

Reference image: `image.png`

This captures the idea that **many bold ideas unfold like pages in a book** — continuous, tactile, exploratory, multi-generational.
