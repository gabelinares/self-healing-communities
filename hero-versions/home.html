Why it matters

It begins with seeing differently.

Decades of research, starting with the ACE Study, show how early experience sets off cascades across a whole life. When we understand what interrupts a community's healing, we can act to restore it.

Scroll to turn the book.

Experience sets off cascades.

Early experiences ripple across a whole life, for better and for worse. The ACE Study made those connections impossible to ignore.

Adversity carries forward.

Left unaddressed, early adversity resurfaces later as poor health, and it passes from one generation to the next.

We can change the conditions.

When we see what interrupts a community's healing, we can restore the conditions that let each generation grow stronger than the last.

How it works

We don't fix one problem at a time.

The Fund's work rests on a single shift. One way leans on people to compensate, fixing what's broken one piece at a time. The other restores the conditions of the living system, so wellbeing can flow. Drag the handle: it's the same community, blocked or connected.

A living system, restored

  • Relationships, resources, feedback loops
  • Health and connection flow
  • People grow stronger together
  • Stronger with each generation

Leaning on constant rescue.
