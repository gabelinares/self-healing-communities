How it works

We don't fix one problem at a time.

The Fund's work rests on a single shift. One way leans on people to compensate, fixing what's broken one piece at a time. The other restores the conditions of the living system, so wellbeing can flow. Drag the handle: it's the same community, blocked or connected.
